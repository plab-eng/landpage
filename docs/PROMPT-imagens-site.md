# Prompts das imagens — home, Plugins e P-LAB OS

Mesmo estilo de `docs/PROMPT-imagens-servicos-bim.md`: comece TODOS os
prompts pelo bloco "Estilo comum" daquele arquivo. Salve em `images/` com o
nome indicado, em `.webp`, e avise.

**Ilustração, nunca tela falsa.** Nenhum prompt aqui desenha interface do
Revit, do Navisworks, do Civil 3D ou do P-LAB OS: onde o produto existe, o
lugar é de print ou vídeo real. Os itens 2 a 5 são provisórios — quando o
vídeo do botão for gravado, ele entra no lugar.

Proporção: **16:9 (1600 × 900)**, exceto a home.

---

## 1. Home, imagem do topo — `mockup-bim.webp` (substitui a atual; 1600 × 1134)

> [estilo comum] One central isometric multi-storey building model on a
> square platform, half solid and half wireframe with pipes and conduits
> visible inside. Around it, four floating elements connected to the building
> by green circuit lines with glowing gold nodes: (1) a small toolbar of
> simple square tool icons, standing for the Revit add-in; (2) a rounded chat
> bubble with three dots, standing for the AI assistant; (3) a browser window
> frame showing a tiny 3D model and a QR code square, standing for the web
> viewer; (4) a dashboard panel with bar charts and a project list made of
> blank lines, standing for office management. No text, no letters, no
> logos.

## 2. Plugins — Cota Automática — `ex-cota-automatica.webp`

> [estilo comum] An isometric floor plan of a small house seen from above at
> an angle, with clean dimension lines and arrows appearing automatically
> along the walls and columns, as if drawn by an invisible hand; a few
> dimension lines are still being drawn, shown as dashed. Gold nodes at the
> dimension ends.

## 3. Plugins — Gerar Vistas — `ex-gerar-vistas.webp`

> [estilo comum] An isometric house model on the left; to the right, a neat
> fan of identical small view frames flies out of it — floor plans, an
> enlarged bathroom detail and two building sections — each frame a simple
> line drawing. Green lines connect each room of the model to its view.

## 4. Plugins — Gerenciador de Pranchas — `ex-gerenciador-pranchas.webp`

> [estilo comum] A row of blank drawing sheets with identical title blocks
> (only empty boxes, no text), and small view frames (plans and sections)
> sliding from a stack into the right place on each sheet, aligned to a
> faint grid. Green lines guide each view to its sheet.

## 5. Plugins — Transferir Circuitos — `ex-transferir-circuitos.webp`

> [estilo comum] Two electrical distribution panels mounted on a wall, drawn
> in isometric line art, each with rows of breakers. A bundle of colored
> circuit wires (green and gold) lifts out of the left panel and lands in
> the right panel in one smooth arc; a small curved "undo" arrow floats
> above. No text.

## 6. Navisworks — Clash Works — `navisworks-clash-works.webp`

> [estilo comum] An isometric cut-away of a ceiling plenum where a duct, a
> beam and several pipes cross; each clash point is marked with a glowing
> gold ring, and the marked points are gathered by green lines into three
> small grouped clusters on the right, like folders sorted by floor.

## 7. Navisworks — Quantificação para o P-LAB OS — `navisworks-quantificacao.webp`

> [estilo comum] An isometric building model whose elements (walls, slabs,
> pipes) emit small flat tokens that travel along green lines into a neat
> stack of table rows and a coin stack on the right — quantities becoming
> cost. Only shapes, no numbers or text.

## 8. Civil 3D — Verificador de traçado — `civil3d-verificador-tracado.webp`

> [estilo comum] An isometric terrain with a railway or road alignment
> winding across it; along the curves, small circular badges sit on each
> curve — most with a check mark, two with a gold warning ring — and thin
> radius lines show the geometry of each curve.

## 9. Civil 3D — Distribuição de terraplenagem — `civil3d-terraplenagem.webp`

> [estilo comum] An isometric terrain cross-section along a road: cut areas
> (hills being lowered) in pale gold and fill areas (valleys being raised) in
> soft green, with curved arrows carrying earth from each cut to a fill, and
> a small mass-haul curve floating above the terrain. No text.

## 10. P-LAB OS — Dimensionamento (no horizonte) — `plab-os-dimensionamento.webp`

> [estilo comum] An isometric stretch of drainage and water pipes with
> manholes, each pipe segment tagged with a small blank label; a floating
> calculation panel (only shapes: slider, gauge, check mark) is connected to
> the pipes by green lines, as if sizing them.
